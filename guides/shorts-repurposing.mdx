# Shorts Repurposing Studio

Convert approved 16:9 long-form videos into engaging 9:16 YouTube Shorts with subtitle burning and dynamic framing.

## Layout Options

- **Blurred Background**: Centers 16:9 video over a blurred filled 9:16 background.
- **Center Crop**: Intelligently crops key subject in 9:16 frame.
- **Stacked Layout**: Places main scene on top with auto-generated visuals or captions below.

## Subtitle Burning

The Shorts Repurposing Studio utilizes FFmpeg to auto-sync subtitle timings and burn animated text overlays directly onto the video.
