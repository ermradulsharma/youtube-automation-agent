# Approval Workflow & Quality Gates

YouTube Automation Agent is built **approval-first** to ensure content quality and brand safety.

## Approval States

Every generated video item passes through clear pipeline statuses:

1. `DRAFTED`: Script generated by Script Writer Agent.
2. `RENDERED`: Video assembly and TTS narration completed by Production Agent.
3. `PENDING_APPROVAL`: Held in dashboard quality gate awaiting human review.
4. `APPROVED`: Verified by human operator or auto-approved by policy.
5. `SCHEDULED` / `PUBLISHED`: Handled by Publishing Agent.

```
Drafted ➔ Rendered ➔ Pending Approval ➔ [Human Review Gate] ➔ Approved ➔ Published
```

## Approval Options in Dashboard

- **Approve**: Queues the video for immediate or scheduled YouTube upload.
- **Reject & Regenerate**: Triggers scene-specific or full video regeneration with custom notes.
- **Edit Metadata**: Allows manual adjustments to titles, tags, description, or thumbnails.
