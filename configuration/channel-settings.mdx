# Channel Settings

Define target niche parameters and publishing preferences for your channel.

## Target Niche & Voice Guidelines

Configure channel settings in `config/channel.json`:

- **Niche**: E.g. Tech News, AI Tutorials, History Documentaries.
- **Tone**: Professional, Energetic, Casual, Dramatic.
- **Default Privacy**: `private`, `unlisted`, or `public`.
- **Category ID**: Standard YouTube Category ID (e.g. `28` for Science & Technology).
