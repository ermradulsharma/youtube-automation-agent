# Scene Repair Engine

If a specific scene in a rendered video fails quality review (e.g. awkward AI artifact or bad audio alignment), the Scene Repair Engine allows targeted replacement.

## How Scene Repair Works

1. Open the video item in the Web Dashboard.
2. Select the specific timestamp or scene block.
3. Click **Repair Scene**.
4. Enter optional custom visual or prompt instructions.
5. The Production Agent re-renders only that scene and re-stitches the final video via FFmpeg.
